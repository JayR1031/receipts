This is a conflict between the spec's example and the pre-existing fuzzy threshold for short quotes. It does not show the numbers rule is wrong: both sides yield the token `$19.99`.

The same quote against a page with `$19.49` and `$99` gives `numbers differ ($19.99 not on page; partial_ratio=91.1)`. I rated this LOW (finding 4).
