## Findings

That pad carries wrong prices past a word price to the next plan's (#1). It also rejects true quotes whose added word ties onto page text past the price (#2). Head is worse than `7363a43` on both, and the record shows neither. No measured pad variant is better on every row, so fixing it is a code change or an owner decision at the measured rates.
